# What You Repeat: Suno paste sheet

- **Length target:** about 4:15
- **Alternative titles:** ¿Cómo Estás? (What You Repeat), Lived Daily, a Heartbeat, Otra Vez, Why-llarta, The Next Repetition

If you Extend from the separately made heartbeat sample, delete this whole [Intro] block, because the sample already is the intro.
- Verse 1, 0:15-0:46, 16 bars. Half-spoken and deadpan over rolling sub bass, Rhodes stabs and the beatbox hat groove. Kept sparse so the jokes land (the bay as a soprano, the sandals, the corruption theory, the toddler going vroom). 'And me, for now' starts the self-implication early.
- Pre-Chorus 1, 0:46-1:02, 8 bars. The filter opens, strings swell, and a snare roll and arpeggio rise. The lead moves from speaking to singing on 'It asks me what I repeat', which hands the word 'repeat' to the chorus.

## Song-specific Suno tips

- Your voice: in Voices, record a new sample of your own voice and complete the spoken verification and the rights checkbox. Make it 30-60 s, dry, with no music or reverb, in a quiet room, saved as WAV or MP3. Sing the chorus ('You are not what you say / You are what you repeat / Said once, it's a sentence / Lived daily, a heartbeat') at about 124 BPM around F minor. Sing '¡Hola, buenos días! ¿Cómo estás?' once so the Voice learns your Spanish vowels. Then speak two verse lines in your normal dry delivery, so it learns both your sung and your half-spoken tone. The official minimum is 15 s, but clips under about 20 s often fail.
- Model: start on v6 (Pro/Premier) for the most polished club mix. It is not confirmed that Voices work on the v6 family, so after selecting your Voice, check that it stays active in the model dropdown. If it greys out, use whichever model the dropdown allows with the Voice, and keep 'male lead' in the style only as a fallback.
- Sliders (under Advanced Options): Variety Off/0, so v6 doesn't rewrite 'beatbox' and 'heartbeat' out of the style. Style Influence 70-75%. Weirdness 50-55% for 'something different' that keeps the song form. For a wilder alternate take, try v6-wild at Weirdness about 60. Don't type slider values into the Style box; they would be read as style words.
- Heartbeat start: upload the separately made 124 BPM heartbeat intro, choose Extend from the end of the clip, and paste this style. In the lyrics for that extension, delete the whole [Intro] block and start at [Verse 1], because the clip already is the intro. Set Audio Influence to 75-85% so the tempo and pulse carry into the song. If you generate without the upload, keep the [Intro] block as written.
- Heartbeat end: Suno won't reliably finish on a solo heartbeat. Once you have a good take, place the heartbeat clip after the [Outro] section in Studio (Premier) or any DAW, and fade the music under it. If the take runs on after the outro, use the editor's Fade Out at the end.
- Beatbox throughout: the style prompt carries the beatbox layer, every chorus tag now says 'beatbox', and the parentheses make your voice perform it (boom-tss, ka-tss, ba-dum). If Suno sings 'boom-tss' as words or spells it out, regenerate first. If it keeps happening, change those parentheses to (ba-dum) only, which renders more reliably. For a guaranteed beatbox, record your own 4-bar beatbox loop at 124 BPM and duplicate it across the timeline in Studio.
- Generate 4-6 takes and pick using six checks. Is the first sound a heartbeat with no vocal on it? Do the chorus echoes alternate between lead and crowd? Is Verse 3 actually chanted rather than skipped as an instrumental break? Is the breakdown whispered over only the heartbeat? Does the heartbeat then race into a stop and the drop? Does the final chorus lift in key?
- Key change: the tag [Final Chorus: key change up] is not guaranteed to transpose. If the final chorus doesn't lift, use v6's plain-language section edit on that section, e.g. 'raise this final chorus a whole step to G minor, add disco strings, keep the lyrics', rather than regenerating the whole song.
- Length: the bar plan lands at about 4:15, plus whatever drop padding Suno adds. If Duration offers Custom, set about 4:20. If a take runs past 4:30, cut in this order: '(that's my line)', the second 'Otra vez' tag line, 'Belonging's not a mood (it's a loop)', then the group-chat line. Trim any tail with Fade Out after [End].
- Moderation fallbacks: if 'Eurovision' in the style is flagged or stripped, replace it in both style fields with 'Euro-pop song-contest grand-final anthem'. If the meat-dress line is flagged, replace it with 'And the dance-floor diva too'. Avoid any nickname, because moderation can flag those as well as names. Never put a real artist name in the style box.
- Paste the exclude list into the Exclude field, the first item under Advanced Options. Don't write 'no reggaeton' in the Style box, because that adds the word to the style. The Spanish lyrics can pull a generation toward Latin or tropical styles, which is why latin pop, tropical house, reggaeton and dembow are excluded.
- Spanish pronunciation: keep the accents exactly as written (días, estás, tú, también, Qué, está económico); they help Suno stress the right syllable. If a take anglicizes a phrase, regenerate or section-edit that part. Leave 'Ya soy local' alone: English-flavored stress helps that joke.
- Respell only if a take gets it wrong, and only in Suno's lyrics box; keep the correct spelling in the published lyrics. If 'bow' comes out rhyming with 'go', which breaks the bow/now rhyme, type 'bough'. If 'Vallarta' comes out as va-LAR-ta, type 'Vayarta'.
- Bridge asides: if a take buries '(That would have been honest)', remove its parentheses so the lead sings it. The Gaga hedge 'After I heard her first' is already a lead line, so it can't get lost.
