# La Loca Tlacuaque de la Calle Cerrada

A comic diss track in Mexican street slang about the possum (*la tlacuaque*) that broke into the house on Calle Cerrada. She came in through the roof beam, sent two glass apples to the graveyard, missed the toilet by two inches, peed in the sink, fled to the pool, came back at the last second, got locked in and hid under the sofa. Then la patrulla verde showed up with four Cocas and one pollo, and left with the four Cocas, the pollo and one loca tlacuaque.

Hard, raspy Monterrey-style barrio rap in the verses; a bouncy schoolyard "¡Culón, colito!" jingle for the hook. 90 BPM, D minor.
