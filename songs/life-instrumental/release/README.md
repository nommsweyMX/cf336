# "The Next Repetition"

An instrumental in eleven scenes, about five minutes, that follows the life your book tells (*WHY-LLARTA*), plus the possum night. Everything is synthesized by the code in [`../production/`](../production/); there are no samples and no voices.

**This is a first full draft.** It was built from the book only: that is all I know about your life, so childhood, family, work details and health are not depicted. Nobody has listened to it; it was checked with meters.

## The themes

- **Your theme (the Builder):** a rising fifth and a stepwise fall, in D minor. It is the main tune of scenes 1 and 8, turns major in scene 3, and comes back rebuilt in scene 9.
- **The place:** the book's six chapter titles, from "¡Hola Buenos Dias!" to "Que tengas un buen día", set as six wordless phrases in the rhythm of those sentences. Marimba calls, your guitar answers.
- **Griff:** five quiet notes on electric piano (D, E, F sharp, E, D). It never changes; everything else adjusts to it.
- **The practice:** a repeating eight-note figure. A machine plays it in the old life; in scene 9 you rebuild it by hand.

## Be aware

- **Draft, unheard.** The balance, the trumpet and the comic timing of the possum scene are the things most likely to need changes once someone listens.
- **Synthesized instruments.** The trumpet, flute and strings are simple synthesis and will sound like it; the guitar, marimba, harp and most effects should hold up better.
- **Some effects are stand-ins.** The cafe murmur is wordless filtered noise, and the "laugh from a doorway" is played on marimba and flute, not voiced.
